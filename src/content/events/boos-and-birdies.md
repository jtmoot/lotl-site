Grab your broomstick, because this one is going to be magical. 🧙‍♀️⛳️ Join us
for our Season Finale at Indian Pond Country Club in Kingston on Tuesday,
October 27: great golf, great food, and all of you together in one room. 🖤

Check-in opens at 12:00 PM and we tee off at 1:00 PM with a 9-hole scramble
shotgun start on both the front and back nine. Every lady gets her own witch
hat at check-in, so come ready for the photo of the season! 📸 Costumes and
team looks are welcome, but the hat is all you need.

On the course, expect spooky twists on your favorite contests, a Trick or Treat
hole where your team draws a card for a treat or a trick, and a few surprises.
👻 Afterward we head up to the Blackstone's Room for a generous harvest dinner,
our Witches' Brew signature cocktail, prizes, raffles, and spooky season
superlatives. 🏆

**$160 per golfer**, including 9 holes with cart, dinner, and all the on-course
contests. Register by Wednesday, October 14 for the early bird price of
**$150**. 🎉 We have room for 72 golfers and expect this to fill fast. Sign up
with your foursome, or register on your own and we will pair you with a great
group.

Even if this season got away from you, you are absolutely still welcome. This
day is about having fun and celebrating a fantastic season with all you lovely
ladies, not about how many rounds you played. Don't be put off by the word
"tournament," just come play and have fun! New to Ladies on the Links?
[Register first](/register), then book your spot.

🙌 Want to volunteer for check-in, contests, or the raffle? Or own a business
that would love to be a hole sponsor or raffle prize donor? Email
[help@ladiesonthelinksgolf.com](mailto:help@ladiesonthelinksgolf.com).
